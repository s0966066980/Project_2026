# Require repeated or reproducible evidence for optimization guidance

Status: accepted

A Daily Optimization Simulation distinguishes Observation Signal from Reference Guidance without inventing a confidence percentage. One or two similar Voice Interaction Evidence records may be reported as an observation but cannot produce a concrete LLM, prompt, or RAG adjustment. Three or more similar records, or a behavior reproducible through synthetic fixtures, may produce reference-only guidance at the seam selected by the Optimization Finding Classification.

The report displays actual record counts and redacted supporting evidence. Contradictory evidence is classified as Insufficient Evidence rather than resolved by majority guess. These thresholds govern informational output only and grant no production mutation or publishing authority.
