# Require synthesized speech output for a successful Voice Turn

Voice assistance is an audible customer mode rather than a text assistant with optional sound. A Voice Turn is completed only when text-to-speech produces playable audio for the assistant reply; bounded retry may recover synthesis, but exhausting it — including a reply that carries no audio — ends the turn as Voice Playback Failure even when generated text or a Voice Order Draft is retained for visible recovery. Whether that audio reached the customer is confirmed by service-level TTS availability and on-site verification rather than per-turn playback bookkeeping: a browser that cannot play delivered audio shows the customer an explicit failure and preserves the text, but does not rewrite the recorded outcome, and a kiosk that disappears mid-playback leaves the turn recorded as completed. This makes TTS readiness part of voice-mode readiness and rejects counting text-only degradation as success, at the cost that a recorded success means speech was produced and delivered rather than heard — a distinction every report built on these records must state.
