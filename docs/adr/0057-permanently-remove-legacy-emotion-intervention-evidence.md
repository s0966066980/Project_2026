# Permanently remove legacy emotion intervention and effectiveness evidence

Status: accepted

The P2 emotion rebuild permanently deletes, without backup, the old intervention and assistance modes, rollout and confidence gates, human-evaluation workflow, effectiveness evidence, voice-influence records, assistance outcomes, and their UI, APIs, code, tables, and existing data. The replacement retains only compatible Emotion Model Profiles, the mutually exclusive customer-analysis mode and clip duration, one-shot Live Admin Emotion Tests, and minimal 30-day Emotion Analysis Records. This extends ADR-0028 from disabling automated intervention to removing its pre-P2 evidence model entirely, because hidden legacy scorecards would preserve a second operational meaning after the simplified advisory-only contract becomes authoritative.
